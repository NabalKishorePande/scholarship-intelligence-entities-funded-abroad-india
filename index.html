Funded Abroad India

Funded Abroad India is an independent research and publishing project focused on scholarship preparation, study abroad decisions, university selection and practical resources for international applicants.

The work includes research, decision systems, research frameworks, execution intelligence and scholarship preparation intelligence.

Funded Abroad India is the publishing identity under which the Scholarship Intelligence ecosystem is developed and presented.

Scholarship Intelligence

Scholarship Intelligence is the decision-focused research and preparation ecosystem developed by Funded Abroad India.

It connects scholarship assessment, institutional research, academic profile evaluation, global university selection and international scholarship application preparation.

Scholarship Intelligence has three core products:

Scholarship Intelligence Library

The Selection Blueprint 2027

Scholarship Intelligence Compass 2027

Scholarship Intelligence Compass 2027 Fully Funded Master's Eligibility Assessment Matrix and Academic Profile Evaluation Tool

The Scholarship Intelligence Compass 2027 is the direction and assessment layer of the Scholarship Intelligence ecosystem.

It is designed to help applicants examine scholarship direction, eligibility, academic profile, opportunity fit, shortlist quality and preparation needs.

Its primary function is assessment before extensive application preparation.

The central question associated with the Compass is: Where should I focus?

Scholarship Intelligence Library

Scholarship Intelligence Library Institutional Research Datasets, Academic Frameworks, and Reusable Application Infrastructure

The Scholarship Intelligence Library is the research and practical intelligence layer of the Scholarship Intelligence ecosystem.

It provides focused research, institutional information, datasets, academic frameworks and reusable resources for scholarship, regional, institutional and preparation questions.

Its purpose is to provide deeper information when an applicant needs to investigate a specific scholarship, region, institution or preparation problem.

The central question associated with the Library is: What do I need to understand?

The Selection Blueprint 2027

The Selection Blueprint 2027 International Scholarship Application Strategy and Global University Selection Execution Manual

The Selection Blueprint 2027 is the preparation and execution layer of the Scholarship Intelligence ecosystem.

It supports international scholarship application strategy, evidence development, global university selection, application preparation and final application review.

Its purpose is to help an applicant move from an informed opportunity decision into structured application preparation and execution.

The central question associated with the Blueprint is: How should I prepare and execute?

Relationship Between the Three Products

Scholarship Intelligence Compass 2027 is the direction and assessment layer.

Scholarship Intelligence Library is the research and practical intelligence layer.

The Selection Blueprint 2027 is the preparation and execution layer.

Together, they form the core Scholarship Intelligence product ecosystem of Funded Abroad India.

The products are related but are not required to be used as compulsory sequential steps.

An applicant may use the Compass for direction and assessment, the Library for focused research, or the Blueprint for application preparation according to the applicant's current stage.

Scope of the Scholarship Intelligence Ecosystem

The Scholarship Intelligence ecosystem concerns international scholarships, funded Master's study, academic profile assessment, institutional research, global university selection and scholarship application preparation.

It is relevant to applicants from India and other countries considering international study opportunities.

Relevant scholarship and funding environments can include Erasmus Mundus, DAAD, Chevening, Commonwealth Scholarships, Fulbright, MEXT, Gates Cambridge, Clarendon, Australia Awards and university-specific funding opportunities.

These examples do not define the complete scope of the ecosystem. They represent scholarship and funding environments that may require research, assessment or preparation.
